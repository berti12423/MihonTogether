# Ideas (brainstorm, 2026-10-06)

Two agents, two angles. Sizes: S (a day or less), M (a few days), L (a week or more). Not planned yet; Berti picks.

## Social, community, growth

1. **Share cards** (S): one tap turns your profile, a finished title, a review or a room recap into an image with your invite link and a QR code, for WhatsApp/Instagram/Discord. Built on the phone; no backend cost.
2. **Invites that survive the APK install** (S–M): the /add page shows a friend code to copy; on first run the app picks it up (or asks "Got an invite?"), sends the friend request and shows the inviter's favorites. No more empty Friends tab for new people.
3. **Recruiter badges** (S): "Brought 1/3/10 friends" plus XP, counted once the newcomer has read 5 chapters.
4. **Reading clubs in group chats** (M): pin a title with a pace ("ch. 1–20 by Sunday"); members see progress bars and "4/6 on track". Uses the shared progress and title matching.
5. **Spoiler-safe chapter threads** (M–L): comments per chapter range, unlocked once you've read that far; "3 friends commented on this chapter" at the chapter end.
6. **Scheduled read-together with RSVP** (S–M): "Friday 21:00, ch. 40", Going/Maybe, a reminder on each phone, one tap to join.
7. **Polls: what do we read next?** (S): 2–5 title cards in a group; the winner can become a club or a scheduled session.
8. **Co-op weekly group goal** (M): "Read 100 chapters together this week", a shared bar, a badge and a streak freeze for everyone.
9. **Nudges** (S): a friend can nudge you when your streak is about to break or you're behind in a club. One a day per pair.
10. **Open rooms** (S): mark a room "open to friends" and it shows on their Friends tab as "join".
11. **Monthly/yearly Wrapped** (M): your chapters, top titles, best streak and reading buddy, built on the phone, ending in share cards.
12. **People from your groups** (S): "Add friend" next to group members you aren't friends with yet.
13. **Cheaper APK downloads** (S): Firebase Hosting traffic is the first thing that will cost money as users grow (about 2 GB per release at 50 updaters); move the APK to a free host (Cloudflare R2, or a public releases-only GitHub repo, which needs Berti's OK).

## Reading and translation

1. **Glossary into the prompt + name suggestions** (S–M): the name list currently only swaps words after translation; sending it to Gemini/DeepL keeps names consistent and fixes the grammar around them, and the engine can suggest new names.
2. **Several slices per Gemini request** (M): free Gemini keys are limited by requests per day; batching 2–4 slices stretches the quota 2–4 times and cuts waiting on long strips.
3. **Translate when downloading** (M): downloaded chapters get translated in the background (Wi-Fi/on-device by default), ready offline.
4. **Share the host's translation in rooms** (M–L): guests see the host's Gemini translation instantly; the group spends one quota.
5. **Follow across different sources** (M): when friends read the same series on different sources, follow by chapter and position ("approximate follow", with consent) instead of chat only.
6. **Resume following automatically** (S): after the app was in the background or the connection dropped, "Back in sync?" or snap back within 2 minutes.
7. **No zoom reset when a translation arrives** (S–M).
8. **Hold to see the original page** (S), and tap a bubble for the original text.
9. **Erase the original text instead of covering it** (L): pages look like real scanlations.
10. **Better Japanese OCR / offline translator model** (L): optional downloads for vertical Japanese and key-free offline translation.
11. **Read the translation aloud** (S–M): on-device text-to-speech in reading order, and TalkBack support.
12. **Quota meter + fallback** (S): when Gemini's quota runs out mid-chapter, offer "continue on this phone" instead of stopping.
13. **Where everyone is in the room** (M): member markers on the page slider, optional automatic "wait for me".
14. **Save a translated chapter** (S): a local CBZ of the translated pages.

## The agents' top picks

- Social: share cards + invite onboarding; reading clubs with polls; scheduled sessions with RSVP + open rooms. Early housekeeping: move the APK off Firebase Hosting.
- Reading: glossary in the prompt; batched Gemini + quota fallback; follow across sources + auto-resume.
