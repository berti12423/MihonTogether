# What's new in Mihon Together

The first section is what the app shows in its update screen (through `apk.json` on the website). Newest first; write for readers, not developers.

## October 9, 2026: My library on the website

- **Read your phone's library on your computer:** open mihontogether.web.app → My library, signed in to the same account. Covers, chapters and a full reader (long strip or pages, right-to-left, arrow keys), and your progress is saved on the phone.
- Pages go straight from your phone to your browser over an encrypted connection; nothing is uploaded to our servers. Works best on the same Wi-Fi, with the app open on the phone.
- Friends → Me: "Library on the web" switch (on) and "Share library with my browser" to keep the phone available for 15 minutes.
- Using AdGuard or a VPN on your phone? Exclude Mihon Together in it, or the browser can't reach the phone. The app tells you when this is needed.
- On the website, "Library" is now called "Shared library" (what your friends see).

## October 7, 2026 (update 2)

- "Check for updates" in More → About, and the update screen now reminds you once a day until you update.
- The weekly ranking shows chapters read this week next to XP.
- Change your avatar, banner, bio and accent colour on the website too (Account → Profile look).
- Website: a friend's "Recent" now shows covers like the library, one per title with the latest chapter, instead of a long list.
- Discord: your status no longer gets stuck. Turning Discord status off clears it at once, and a status left behind by a force-stop is cleared the next time you open the app.
- New data meter in Friends → Me: see how much data reading uses versus Mihon Together's friends features.
- Website: new Terms of use page, and the site is easier to find in search engines.

## October 7, 2026: Mihon Together 1.0 🎉

- Double XP launch week: every chapter gives double XP (and the daily cap doubles) from October 7 to 13 (Israel time).
- Double XP weekends: every Friday and Saturday (Israel time) from now on.
- My stats: a new page (Friends → Me → My stats) with your level, XP, streaks, days read, badges, a 12-week chart, your top titles this month and your reading time. Friends' profiles show their stats too.
- Monthly challenges: three new challenges every month, the same for everyone (this month: read 100 chapters, read on 20 days, read 10 chapters together). Each one gives 100 XP, and finishing all three earns a badge for that month.
- "How XP works": every rule in one place, from XP per chapter to the level curve.

## October 6, 2026 (update 3)

- Nudges 👋: nudge a friend from their profile to keep their streak going. Friends whose streak is about to break get a 👋 button in your friends list. One nudge a day per friend; turn nudge notifications off in the notification settings.
- Invites survive installing the app: the invite page gives a friend code to copy, and when you open the app it offers to add the friend who invited you. Got a code? Enter it in the empty Friends tab.
- New badges for bringing friends: Brought a Friend 📨, Brought 3 Friends 🎉 and Brought 10 Friends 🏰, plus 50 XP each, once your friend has read 5 chapters.
- Drop-in rooms: turn on "Let friends drop in" when reading together, and your friends can join from their Friends tab without an invite.
- See where everyone is: when you read in a room without following, the page slider shows where each friend is in the chapter.

## October 6, 2026 (update 2)

- New accounts now share with friends right away (library, activity, online status, daily stats and more). Anyone can turn things off in Friends → Me → Privacy; existing accounts keep their settings.
- Your own card on the ranking podium now shows your chapter count (it was invisible).
- Privacy: hiding a title now also removes your review of it, and when a friend stops sharing, their reviews and progress disappear from title pages within minutes.
- New switch "Share reading progress" in Friends → Me → Privacy. Progress from incognito sources is never shared.
- Profile pictures on the ranking are only visible for people who are actually on it, and never to someone you blocked.
- Group chats: replies to messages from before you joined (or from people you blocked) show as "Earlier message".
- A failed reply keeps what you typed; a failed reaction no longer shows a send error.
- Smoother Friends screen with big libraries; "40 of 120 chapters read" instead of a confusing chapter label.
- Discord reconnect waits for the reader instead of giving up.

## October 6, 2026

- Chats work like a messaging app: long-press a message to react with any emoji, reply or copy it, or swipe it right to reply. Replies show the message they answer; tap it to jump there. Works in group chats and on the website too.
- No more notifications for the chat you're already looking at.
- Recommending a title in a chat now shows your library as covers. In Friends → Me → Favorites you can pick your favorites by tapping covers.
- "Friends who read this": a title's page shows which friends read it and how far they got, even if they read it on another source.
- Rate and review titles you've read (5 stars plus an optional review). Friends see them on the title page and on your profile. Turn it off in Friends → Me → Privacy ("Reviews").
- Opening a friend's title you don't have searches the source they read it on (or all your sources).
- The ranking got a makeover: the top 3 stand on a podium with their banners, and everyone shows their profile picture. The all-time ranking also shows total chapters.
- Everyone on the global ranking now shows their profile picture and banner there. You can turn this off in Friends → Me → Privacy ("Show my picture and banner on the ranking").
- Friends with a banner show it behind their name in your friends list.
- Discord: your status reconnects by itself after you leave the app and come back (no more "ErrorType 9").
- Fixed: the Extensions tab crashed after the last update.

## October 5, 2026

- New "All time" ranking: the top readers by total XP, in the app and on the website.
- Discord: covers from sites that block Discord (like Comix) now show, your level and the people in your room appear under the chapter, and the Mihon Together logo sits on the cover with a credit on hover. You can hide your level in Settings → Discord.
- Pages that fail with a "busy" error (503) are retried a few times automatically before showing an error.
- Play Protect no longer needs to be turned off: the app stops asking to install apps. Extensions now install inside the app (existing ones keep working), and "Download" on the update screen downloads through your browser; tap the file to install.

## October 4, 2026 (update 2)

- Discord: your status now shows as "Watching {title}", so it doesn't replace a game you're playing. You can switch back to "Playing" in Settings → Discord.
- "Check connection" (Friends → Me) recognizes mobile networks that can't reach Google over IPv6 and shows how to fix it (set the APN protocol to IPv4).

## October 4, 2026

- Join a reading room in one tap: no need to add the title to your library first.
- Start reading together from a friend's profile, a title card or a group chat.
- Notifications open the right chat, group or room, with Reply, Accept and Join buttons.
- "Let's read this": mark titles in a friend's library; if they mark the same one, you both get a match.
- After reading together, the chat shows a short recap.
- Friends is split into Friends, Chats and Me tabs.
- Translation: text is fitted into the speech bubbles, whole chapters translate ahead, you can fix a line by hand, keep a name list per title, and quote a bubble into the room chat.
- Translation stops properly when a cloud quota runs out, and the language pack downloads on Wi-Fi only (unless you allow mobile data).
- A short Mihon Together setup step when you first open the app.
- Fixed: the app crashed on the first-run setup screen after a fresh install.
- New: Friends → Me → "Check connection" shows which online service fails (helps with mobile-data problems).

## October 3, 2026

- Group chats with up to 20 friends.
- XP, levels and 15 achievements; a weekly XP ranking among friends; streak freezes.
- Reading rooms: "Ready" and "Wait for me" buttons.
- Recommend a title in any chat as a cover card.
- Free translation on your phone (no key, no limits); DeepL, Papago and Gemini stay as options.
- Fixed: Comix chapters failing with HTTP 403.
- Fixed: the Discord status now goes away as soon as you leave the reader.
- Covers of your library now show on the website for every source.
- Much smaller download (about 43 MB instead of 106 MB).
- The app tells you when an update is ready.
