# Shared reader integration

The reader overflow menu now opens **Read together**. An authenticated reader can confirm the chapter's edition and page order, then invite an accepted friend. The guest accepts the invitation in Friends, opens their matching local title and chooses Follow host. Each device fetches its own pages; only bounded metadata and positions are sent.

`ReadingSessionStore` is the in-memory room handoff from Friends to the reader. The reader Activity's STARTED lifecycle owns room listeners, activity publication, a 30-second host heartbeat and the position writer. Backgrounding freezes following immediately and stops renewals. Finishing the reader attempts activity removal and room leave; offline removal is visibly pending and the server activity/room expiries remain the fallback.

Pages are offered only after an actual reader page becomes ready. One writer has one in-flight transaction and one coalesced latest pending position. The repository also serializes underlying Firebase Tasks across reader recreation. Failed turns are discarded and reconciled with a fresh server read; new reading input is required to publish again. The client does not persist an offline room outbox.

Following requires exact content/source/work/edition/chapter identifiers, the same explicit page-mapping identifier, valid page bounds and manual confirmation for that chapter. Chapter loading resolves the exact chapter from the installed source, then waits for confirmation before moving pages. Equal page counts never imply compatibility. A query-addressed or otherwise unsupported source/chapter locator fails closed; source-specific stable-locator adapters are future work. A missing chapter is shown as a mismatch.

Only authorized server snapshots can request reader moves. The Activity revalidates room ID, epoch, sequence, chapter, expiry, foreground state and consent before calling its existing private page-navigation helper. Duplicate/stale sequence snapshots cannot navigate; turning Follow host off and on allows applying the current snapshot again. Guests never write the room's `position` (only the host's lease does), and the remote viewer callback is fenced against echo. Since 2026-10-06 members share their own place separately (see the last section); it never moves anyone's reader.

Source-specific incognito changes, hidden titles, invisible mode and unsupported identities suppress publishing immediately. A sharing-generation change fences cancelled activity callables before bounded clear/leave attempts. This invalidates older shared library/favorite generations too, requiring deliberate republishing. Account changes clear the room handoff and mapping consent. Own accepted-friend edges are observed explicitly, so removing/blocking the other participant clears room state even if an existing Firestore room listener has not yet reported an authorization error.

`ReadingProtocolTest` covers missing consent, source/edition/chapter/page-order mismatches, invalid page bounds, stale/duplicate epochs/sequences, viewer echo, rapid-turn coalescing and failure queue discard. `ReadingTogetherCoordinatorTest` drives the coordinator with a mocked repository on an immediate dispatcher: re-enabling Follow host applies the current snapshot, an unreadable room ends as EXPIRED without a raw error, and cache-only (offline) accepted edges pause the room while only a server snapshot without the edge ends it. Native compilation and Gradle results are recorded in the implementation checkpoint.

Behaviour confirmed on two emulators (2026-10-02, [evidence](evidence/TWO_DEVICE_READER.md)): a followed chapter change loads the exact chapter at page 1 and waits for confirmation; a network outage shows "waiting" and keeps the room, and Follow host must be turned on again afterwards (as after backgrounding); lease expiry, the other participant leaving, or friend removal end the room on both sides; participants can always leave or close a room, even after its lease expired. Real phones and a production Firebase project are still unverified.

## Room guidance and chat-only (2026-10-02)

- Joining a room (Friends → Join) resolves the room's title in the local library by source edition (`RoomDestinations`) and opens the reader at the host's chapter, or the title page when the chapter isn't known locally. Titles not in the library show "Find it" (global search by title).
- Page-order consent is per edition (source + edition) for the reader session: inviting from a chapter, or tapping Follow in the prompt, gives it once; later chapters of the same edition don't ask again. The dialog's confirm button only appears for `CONFIRM_MAPPING`.
- A guest on the room's title who isn't following sees a prompt: Follow (consent + follow in one tap) or Just chat. On another title the status is `ELSEWHERE`: the room, chat and reactions stay; the prompt names the host's title and chapter with Go there (reopens the reader on it when it's in the library) or Just chat. The emote panel can switch between Follow and Just chat.
- Privacy is unchanged: incognito, hidden titles and invisible mode still leave the room.

## Open rooms and member positions (2026-10-06)

- **Let friends drop in.** The Read together dialog has a switch, off by default: for the room I host it calls `open`; before a room exists it is passed with `create`. Friends who see my activity get a card on their Friends tab ("Alex is reading X", people count, Join) with a consent line like group invites have (people in the room may not be their friends, and they will see the joiner's page). Join selects the room and opens the title the way an invite does. Any privacy change (incognito, hidden title, invisible mode, sharing switches) bumps the generation, which closes the room to drop-ins; a host hand-off closes it too. The web shows the same cards with "Join chat".
- **Where everyone is.** As a joined member who isn't the host, the coordinator shares my chapter key and page index with `position` (`publishMyPlace`). The latest place wins, at most once every 3.5 s on the client (the server allows 3 s), and only while I read the room's title in the room's edition. Any other title sends one clear, so another title's chapter never reaches the room. Nothing is sent in the background or while incognito. Failures are dropped; the next page turn tries again.
- **Slider markers.** While I'm in a room and **not following**, the reader's page slider shows a small initial in each member's accent for everyone on exactly my chapter: the host from the room's `position`, members from their shared `page` (ignored after 10 minutes or beyond my chapter's page count). `RoomMarkers.place` decides. Following hides them, since the host's position already drives the view. Marker placement is approximate (it assumes the slider's thumb inset), and the vertical navigator assumes page 1 at the top.
- Tests: `RoomPositionsTest` (marker placement, what I share, open-room card list); backend domain tests for drop-in joins and positions; an emulator rules test for `openRooms` and member positions.
